What programs does Glowberry Utopia offer?+

We offer Daycare, Nursery, LKG, and UKG. Our team can help you explore the right program for your child and explain current eligibility and availability.

What is your learning approach?+

Our curriculum is Montessori and Reggio Emilia-inspired. The COGS-LIFE Learning Framework brings together eight areas of development, from movement and independence to social and emotional skills.

What ages, timings, and fees apply?+

Please contact our team for the current age requirements, program timings, daycare options, fees, and availability. We’ll help you understand the options for your family.

Can we visit before deciding?+

Yes—select Book a visit to open our enquiry form. Share your preferred day and questions, then send your enquiry through WhatsApp or email. Our team will confirm the arrangements with you.
