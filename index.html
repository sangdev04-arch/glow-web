About Glowberry Utopia

Glowberry Utopia is a preschool and daycare on Varthur Main Road in Gunjur, led by IIT and Symbiosis alumni.

Our program gives children time to explore materials, move, play together and practise everyday tasks. We use the COGS-LIFE framework to support skills from coordination to independence and social development.

How children learn

Our curriculum draws on Montessori and Reggio Emilia.

Hands-on exploration

Children sort, build and test ideas with materials they can handle themselves.

Creative expression

Children use drawing, stories and pretend play to communicate what they notice and imagine.

Working together

Shared activities give children practice listening, taking turns and solving problems together.

Explore the COGS-LIFE framework

The COGS-LIFE learning framework

Our framework covers eight areas of development, including control of objects, outdoor and nature connection, gross motor skills, social development, locomotor abilities, independent work, fine motor skills, and emotional and life skills.

Control of Objects

Exploring how things move, fit, and work together helps children build coordination and discover cause and effect.

Activity example

Stack blocks, fit shapes into a sorter or roll a ball.

What a school day looks like

Stories and conversation

Building and sorting

Art and pretend play

Movement
