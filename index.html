Welcome to KEYS Gunjur

A thoughtful beginning makes all the difference.

The early years are full of firsts: the first question, the first friendship, the first “I did it myself!”. At KEYS, we want those moments to feel joyful, meaningful and full of possibility.

Our learning experiences invite children to observe, experiment, communicate and build confidence—without rushing childhood.

How learning feels here

Less “sit still”.
More “let’s find out”.

Children make sense of the world by trying things, talking about them and trying again. We aim to make room for that natural process through a balance of guided activities, free exploration and connection.

👀

Notice

Look closely, wonder and ask questions.

👐

Explore

Use materials, movement and imagination.

💬

Express

Share ideas through words, stories and art.

Grow
